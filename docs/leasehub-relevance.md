# General relevance to property-management engineering

Property finance teams need to turn incompatible source exports into trustworthy operational records. PortfolioLedger demonstrates this general engineering problem with two invented formats, explicit source identity, revision handling, transaction-safe retries and evidence-backed reconciliation. Its separate monthly administration-fee preview demonstrates effective dates, eligibility and reproducible Decimal calculations without pretending to calculate insurance coverage or actual premiums.

Durable worker leases, bounded retries, PostgreSQL constraints and append-only corrections are broadly relevant to operational integration work, including landlord onboarding, reconciliation and recovery-oriented exception review. The review workflow captures a human rationale and actor without automatically changing financial history.

This project is an independent synthetic-data portfolio demonstration. It is not LeaseHub and claims no affiliation, client usage, proprietary knowledge, real integration, endorsement or partnership with LeaseSurance, Octoco, MRI, Xero, Guardrisk or any property-management provider. No insurance claims adjudication or recovery case tracker has been implemented. Relevance refers only to common engineering responsibilities, not a statement about any organization's internal architecture.
